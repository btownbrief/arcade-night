Host recipe
  1. Laptop to the TV: HDMI cable (any laptop, USB-C adapter if needed), or AirPlay from a Mac to an Apple TV. Set the TV to that input.
  2. Open the screen link above in Chrome or Safari, press F for fullscreen. It rotates on its own.
  3. Show the QR (it is on the screen every rotation) and say: scan it, pick a game, play on your phone.
  4. At the end the screen switches to the trophy itself. Press Space to reveal early, H for the host panel.
  5. Take the photo of the winner with the screen behind them. The share line is on the finale screen.

Rehearse: screen in demo mode · phone page in demo mode. Nothing is saved anywhere; the night lives in the link.
